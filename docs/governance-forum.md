# Governance Forum

The Quorum governance forum provides a structured, asynchronous space for pre-proposal discussion before moving to on-chain voting. This complements real-time chat (Discord) with a threaded, searchable format appropriate for serious governance proposals.

## Forum Platform

We use **GitHub Discussions** as our governance forum:
- [Governance Proposals](https://github.com/StellarQuorum/StellarQuorum/discussions/categories/governance-proposals) — For formal proposal drafts seeking community feedback
- [Governance Questions](https://github.com/StellarQuorum/StellarQuorum/discussions/categories/governance-questions) — For informal questions and idea exploration

## When to Use the Forum

### Use the Forum For:
- Drafting formal governance proposals (parameter changes, treasury decisions, protocol upgrades)
- Seeking structured feedback on complex proposals
- Temperature checks before investing in on-chain proposal creation
- Archival discussion that should be searchable by future contributors

### Use Discord For:
- Real-time coordination and quick questions
- Community announcements and social interaction
- Casual brainstorming and networking

## Proposal Discussion Process

### 1. Draft Your Proposal
Start by drafting your proposal in the forum using the **Governance Proposals** category. Include:
- Clear title describing the proposal
- Background and motivation
- Detailed specification of changes
- Expected impact and risks
- Timeline (if applicable)

### 2. Gather Feedback
Engage with comments and iterate on your draft based on community feedback. The forum format allows for:
- Threaded replies on specific points
- Clear attribution of feedback
- Searchable history for reference

### 3. Temperature Check
Before moving on-chain, ensure:
- Your draft has been posted for at least 7 days
- You've addressed major concerns raised in comments
- There's rough consensus on the proposal direction

### 4. Move to On-Chain Proposal
Once your draft has matured through forum discussion:
1. Finalize the proposal text based on feedback
2. Follow the [Proposal Style Guide](proposal-style-guide.md) for the on-chain submission
3. Create the on-chain proposal through the Quorum governance interface
4. Link the on-chain proposal in the forum discussion for transparency

## Forum Moderation

- Keep discussions civil and on-topic (see [Code of Conduct](../CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md))
- Duplicate proposals may be merged or closed with a reference to the original
- Moderators may pin important discussions or categorize them for better discoverability

The governance forum is one part of our community infrastructure:
- **README** — Links to both the forum and Discord
- **Discord** — Real-time chat and announcements
- **Forum** — Structured, searchable proposal discussion
- **On-chain** — Final binding governance decisions
