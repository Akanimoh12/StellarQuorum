## How to Add Your Protocol

To add your protocol to this list:

1. **Fork the repository** and create a branch following the [contributing guidelines](CONTRIBUTING.md)
2. **Add a row** to the table above with:
   - **Protocol**: Your protocol/organization name (link to your website)
   - **Description**: A 1-2 sentence description of what your protocol does
   - **Governance Use Case**: Brief description of how you use Quorum (e.g., treasury management, parameter tuning, upgrade proposals)
3. **Open a pull request** with the title "docs: add [protocol name] to ADOPTERS.md"

Your PR will be reviewed and merged by the maintainers. We reserve the right to edit descriptions for clarity and consistency.

## Reference Deployments

Adopters are encouraged to share their governance configurations (quorum thresholds, voting periods, timelock durations) as reference implementations for new protocols. If you'd like to share your configuration details, include them in your PR description.
